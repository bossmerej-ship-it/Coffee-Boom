Location & Atmosphere

Our coffee bar, located on the first floor of the Astana Tower BC in the bustling Samal 12 neighborhood, offers a cozy space for studying, business meetings, and family brunch. The cafe accommodates guests with 12 seats inside and 12 on the terrace. Guests can enjoy fast, free Wi-Fi and tables for working on laptops, as well as a seasonal summer terrace.
