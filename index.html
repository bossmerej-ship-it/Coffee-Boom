About Kent Sandwich

Kent Sandwich is a cozy street diner established in Astana, located at Adolf Yanushkevich Street, 1. We specialize in hot, freshly grilled crispy sandwiches prepared on spot with wholesome meat, melted cheese, and signature homemade garlic dressings. Whether you need a quick lunch between university classes or a hearty bite after work, our kitchen prepares delicious meals in less than seven minutes.

Our place features comfortable indoor dining tables accommodating twenty guests, clean seating, and durable takeaway packaging for customers on the go. Each roll is pressed on an authentic contact grill at high temperature until golden brown, ensuring that crunchy bite and juicy filling.
