Express Takeaway Pre-Ordering

Welcome to the digital pre-order service for Coffee Boom at Samal 12, located on the ground floor of Astana Tower. To accommodate busy corporate schedules, morning routines, and quick study intervals, we provide a streamlined pickup service that eliminates standing in queue.

Our kitchen and barista station begin extraction and preparation fifteen minutes before your requested arrival window. All hot beverages are capped in double-walled insulated cups, while freshly baked pastries and warm bakery goods are carefully boxed to retain freshness and ideal serving temperature.
